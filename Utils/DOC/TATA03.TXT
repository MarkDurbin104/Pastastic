   Dear Jeremy

   Re:  extending the contract for the TATA-UNISYS consultants

   Following your request for a one month contract extension for the
   two TATA-UNISYS consultants, I have had discussions with the TATA-
   UNISYS representative.

   The request was made a little late at just one week before the end 
   of the contract.  A great deal of work has been involved in finding
   a way to extend their contract, taking into account their personal 
   circumstances and plans as well as other business commitments.  

   A compromise has had to be reached with respect to the extension.
   The consultants require some time off and will be unable to resume 
   work with iT until Monday 5 August 1991.  The consultants will be 
   travelling back home to India for a period before this date.
   
   Also, the short notice and additional travel arrangements have 
   resulted in an increased charge to UNISYS UK and therefore a  new 
   rate will apply for the extension and subsequent periods.  The 
   rate is 320.00 pounds per consultant per normal working day.  The 
   multipliers applying to overtime work remain the same.

   Please let me know if this arrangement is acceptable to iT.
