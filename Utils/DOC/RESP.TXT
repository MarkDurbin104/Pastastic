6.1.2 The BOP can initiate communication via semaphores in pipes.
5.6.1 I 1992 Mark Durbin & John Englezou set up LAN performance/resilience 
      tests
      With only one CT in circuit our tests show a through-put 
      of 170792 bits/sec (21349 bytes/second)

      With one CT in pass-through mode in circuit between CT and BOP
      our tests show a through-put of 169040 bits/sec (21130 bytes/second)

      With twenty one CT's in pass-through mode in circuit between CT and BOP
      our tests show a through-put of 134000 bits/sec (16750 bytes/second)

      The only reason I can see for the difference between iT's results and 
      those obtained by us, is that in the tests performed by iT 
      LANSAT.SYS & LANSERVE.COM had not had the RETRY-TIMEOUT & RETRY-COUNT
      valuse set to appropriate values.
