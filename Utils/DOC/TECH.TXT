The Multiport comms card has 7 serial ports at addresses.

    3F8     Serial Port 1                  
    2F8     Serial Port 2
    2E8     Serial Port 3
    310     Serial Printer Port (4)
    3E0     Serial Scale Port
    300     Lan in
    260     Lan out

The first two map directly onto the standard AT COM1/COM2 addresses.

The board has a facility for HP Barcode circuitry but this section of
the board is not currently populated.
