a)   ON/OFF

    The OM/OFF switch has three positions:

        1       "power off"     -  OFF
        2       "play"          -  (normal) Play mode
        3       "record"        -  record mode

        In position 3 (record mode) the keyboard can be set to play back
        the a tune recorded by you. 

        If the keyboard has been left switched ON for more than 5 minutes
        without any key presses, the power light will automatically go out
        and the keyboard will switch off.  To switch the keyboard back on
        move the ON/OFF switch to OFF and back to ON.


b)   VOICE SELECT

    The VOICE SELECT switch has four positions:

        1       "normal"
        2       "solo 1"
        3       "solo 2"
        4       "chord"

    When switch (a) is in the "play" mode the position of this switch selects
    between normal play and chord play, where positions 1,2 and 3 have the 
    same effect.

    When switch (a) is in "record" mode the position of this switch selects
    which voice you are currently recording.

        "normal" & "solo 1" both record  the first  voice
        "solo 2"                 records the second voice
        "chord"                  records the chord and rhythm
